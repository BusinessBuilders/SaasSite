01 · The situation

The hard part is already done

Ten years in, licensed and insured, family owned, and a 5.0 rating across 51 Google reviews — in a trade where most outfits fight to stay above 4.2. That reputation is worth real money. The problem is that almost nobody sees it before they call somebody else.

The one number hurting you

Your website is a single page. Every link in your menu — Junk Removal, Cleanouts, Demolition, About — scrolls further down that same page. It never opens a new one.

Google ranks pages, not businesses. When somebody searches "estate cleanout near me" or "hot tub removal Worcester County," Google looks for a page about that exact thing. You have one page trying to be nine things, so it ranks strongly for none of them. A competitor with worse reviews and a dedicated page per service quietly takes the call.

Two more leaks. Your 51 reviews live on Google but never appear on your website, so a visitor sees no proof at all. And you cover a wide rural area from Barre without a single page telling Google which towns you serve.

02 · Straight talk

About being first on Google

We will not promise you the top spot

Google decides rankings, not us. It changes how it decides several times a year and weighs things nobody outside Google controls. Any agency guaranteeing position one is either guessing or lining up someone to blame when it doesn't happen.

What we promise is everything that is in our control — done properly, measured monthly, shown to you in plain numbers.

What we commit to in writing

  • Every service you offer gets its own page, built to be found for that service.
  • Every town you serve gets its own page, so you appear where you actually drive.
  • Every page submitted to Google and confirmed indexed — visible to you in Search Console.
  • Your Google Business Profile filled out completely, which is what drives the map results most of your calls come from.
  • Your 51 reviews pulled onto the site so proof sits where people decide.
  • A baseline ranking report at launch, updated monthly, so movement is measured instead of claimed.

That's the honest deal: we control the work, Google controls the ranking, and you see the scoreboard either way.

And it will not book jobs you can't staff — done right, the constraint becomes truck and crew, not leads.
